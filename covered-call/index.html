01 / THE RULE

Room to rise. Less premium.

Choose the strike

K = min { observed K ≥ 1.05 × S }

At the Monday order time, buy 100 shares only if flat. Then select the smallest actually observed AAPL call strike at or above the 5% target, expiring that Friday. Only quote/print evidence available by entry counts. Existing shares are retained.

Book the trade

Sell exactly one covered call at a simulated limit fill of (BID + ASK) / 2. Both quotes must be valid in the same completed hourly bar as the stock print. Missing or crossed quotes mean no option fill.

Wait through expiry

If Friday’s closing-bar stock print is ≤ K, the call expires at zero and shares remain. If S > K, deliver the 100 shares at K and become flat. No rolls, buy-to-close, early exits, or early assignment.

Observed-universe limitation: historical chain coverage is incomplete. The selected strike is the minimum in captured observations, not a guaranteed minimum across every exchange-listed contract. No intermediate strike is assumed to exist. Inspect the actual candidate universe →

The farther-OTM strike leaves more upside before assignment, in exchange for less premium and less downside cushion. It does not reduce the underlying stock’s downside risk.

03 / NAV & REG T

The covered call adds $0 to the stock’s initial requirement in the assignment’s simplified Reg T model. Entry is rejected if prospective available funds are negative. Subsequent margin breaches are flagged; the no-early-exit rule precludes an invented liquidation.

Weekly decisions and skipped trades

Rejected and unfilled decisions stay outside the blotter. A stock purchase can remain booked even when the option leg is skipped.

05 / ACCOUNT STATE

Where the cash went.

Cash changes only on BUY, SELL, EXPIRE, or ASSIGN. Quotes change marks and NAV, not cash. A missing mark carries the last valid observed mark forward for valuation only, with its original timestamp and a stale flag; it never creates an entry fill.
