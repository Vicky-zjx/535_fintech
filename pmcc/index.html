Finding

The long call required $10,133.15 upfront versus $31,269.13 for 100 shares, before short-premium receipts. On the same $50,000 equity, midpoint PMCC earned $1,841.60 net—$214.94 below buy and hold; quoted-side PMCC earned $1,437.60. Lower upfront outlay is not lower risk: the long call adds time-value and volatility exposure. This short, single-stock sample does not establish a general advantage.

02 / CAPITAL & EXPOSURE

Separate capital from return.

Initial long-position cost · USD

Initial long-call or stock purchase cost, including that long leg’s entry fee or stock slippage, before short-call premiums. Not account NAV or net profit.

Delta-dollar exposure · USD

Option exposure uses historical DELTA from the corresponding date, combined with any stock position. It measures sensitivity to stock-price changes, not cash balance or P&L.

P&L attribution and account formula

NAV = economic cash + long-call MV − short-call liability + signed stock MV + dividend receivables − dividend payables − accrued borrow. Trade-date proceeds are included once, not duplicated as settlement receivables. Settled cash and broker buying power are unavailable. No stock Reg T formula is applied to PMCC. Delta-dollar exposure is unavailable without valid date-specific deltas.

05 / METHOD & REPRODUCIBILITY
Inspect the implementation.

    
Trading rules, timing, fills and costs
    
Prior-session inputs are checked at 09:00 ET on the first actual trading day of each week. Simulated execution and daily valuation use the close to match the daily input frequency; overnight data availability is assumed where publication times are unknown. No future quotes establish earlier candidate membership.

    
DecisionImplemented rule

      
Signal → executionPrevious session EOD inputs → next scheduled first-session close. 09:00 ET signal checkpoint; publication availability must be known or explicitly assumed. Normal 16:00 ET / actual early close, with DST.

      
Daily close referenceValid daily BID/ASK first. If a daily leg is absent, the last complete quote event within the final 60 seconds may supply the mark and unchanged midpoint / quoted-side simulated fill. Both source and event times must be same-day, pre-close and within 60 seconds. No stale-bar carry, trade substitution or null-to-zero. Actual quote time stays separate from modeled closing time.

      
Long365–550 signal DTE; expiry nearest 450 then earlier. Mode fixed before P&L: 75%-of-spot strike proxy. Largest observed strike ≤ 0.75 × signal spot, valid signal quote. Replace at weekly decision when remaining DTE ≤ 90.

      
Weekly shortMinimum strike among historically evidenced Friday-series candidates with K ≥ 1.05 × signal spot and ≥ long K; shorter expiry, compatible assumed standard terms. No intermediate strikes. Scheduled last-trading date is derived from contract rules and the historical calendar.

      
Fill sequenceLock both legs at signal. Resolve old obligations → buy new long → permit preselected short. Missing short quote retains long. No re-ranking or midweek retry. Positive bid required when supplied.

      
AssignmentExpiry ≥ $0.01 ITM: cash +100K, stock −100, long retained. Cover next session close. No same-close discretionary re-entry. Announced pre-ex dividend: ITM and max(mid − intrinsic, 0) < dividend.

      
Costs$0.65 per option per side; stock 1 bp per side, $0 commission; short stock 3% annually over calendar days; exercise/assignment fee $0; idle cash 0%.

      
Cash & terminalOne unit, no discretionary borrowing. Paired long sale/stock cover only if required and affordable. Terminal closing liquidation precedes after-close assignment; missing mandatory prices leave unresolved exposure.

    

  

    
Contract catalogue, term assumptions and exclusions
    
Multiplier and deliverable are explicitly labeled research assumptions for ordinary AAPL OPRA contracts. They are supported by OCC standard specifications, available LSEG descriptions and the corporate-action screen—not individual historical supplier certification. Apple’s split history, the 2020 OCC adjustment memo and OIC adjustment guidance inform that screen; no full historical OCC-memo completeness claim is made.

    

    
Identity/expiry follow LSEG’s option RIC rules. Zero-padded days follow the instructor’s correction and retained paired tests. A historical quote demonstrates existence by its availability time; current discovery alone does not. Last observed quote is an observation, not a contractual date.
