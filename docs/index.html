03 / THE PROCESS

From the nightly book to the next session.

The Wheel, one phase at a time

  1. Cash → short put

    Sell one OTM put and reserve strike × 100 in cash. The premium is a receipt, not the strategy’s net profit.

  2. Put assignment → shares

    If assigned, buy 100 shares at the strike. If the put expires without assignment, wait for the next weekly put-entry session.

  3. Shares → covered call

    After Sanji confirms the stock position, evaluate a call for the next session. Keep at least 100 unencumbered shares behind it.

  4. Call assignment → cash

    If called away, return to the cash phase. Otherwise retain shares and evaluate another qualifying call.

The book is authoritative

Sanji owns fills, expiry and assignment. This algorithm reads the updated book; it never edits positions or invents fills. Existing short options and pending orders prevent duplicate exposure.

No rolls, stop-losses, naked calls or borrowing. Stock losses can exceed all premiums collected.

04 / ENTRY & CONTRACT SELECTION

Explicit rules. Observable inputs.

New puts are planned only for the first actual XNYS session of the week, including holiday-adjusted Tuesdays. Calls may be planned for any next session after confirmed assignment. All signals use the latest completed session; a run during an open session is blocked.

01

Trend alignment

Close ≥ SMA50
SMA20 ≥ SMA50

SMAn = mean of the last n completed-session closes. Avoid initiating bullish exposure against a weakening trend; recoveries may be missed.

Illustration only: close 50, SMA20 49 and SMA50 48 pass.

02

Volatility ceiling

HV20 ≤ 45%

Sample standard deviation (ddof = 1) of 20 daily log returns × √252. Requires 21 closes. Historical volatility is not implied volatility.

Illustration only: HV20 of 38% passes; 46% fails.

03

Sudden-drop protection

R5 ≥ −8%

R5 = Closet / Closet−5 − 1. Avoid opening puts after sharp declines; the rule can also miss a rebound.

Illustration only: 50 → 46 is −8%, at the boundary.

These initial thresholds are risk-motivated, not statistically optimized. Fifty consecutive valid session closes are required. Put trend, volatility and earnings filters are not automatic call-entry requirements.

Cash-secured puts

  • Actual observed OTM puts, 3–10 calendar DTE measured from the order’s for date; prefer the eligible expiry closest to 7 days (earlier on a tie).
  • Closest absolute delta to 0.25 when the eligible expiry slice has reliable same-session Greeks. Ties go further OTM.
  • Incomplete Greeks: nearest observed strike to 97% of the closing price. This fallback is not a 25-delta estimate.
  • No earnings from entry through expiry, inclusive. Unknown, empty or provisional earnings information blocks a new put.

Covered calls

  • At least 100 shares, no covering call or conflicting pending order; one contract per ticker.
  • Same 3–10 DTE range and +0.25 delta target.
  • Strike must meet both 1.01 × known stock cost basis and 1.02 × latest close.
  • Without reliable Greeks, select exactly two observed strikes above ATM and verify both price floors. If this fails, skip; do not silently move the strike.
What counts as a usable contract?

LSEG discovery supplies the current observed candidates; it is not treated as a complete historical chain. Ordinary OPRA RICs are cross-checked against strike, type, expiry and underlying metadata. Standard, American, USD and 100-share lot metadata must be present. Adjusted/mini or conflicting descriptions are excluded. No synthetic strike grid is used.

Historical DELTA_EXCH must carry the signal-session date. An undated Option Watch delta is not relabeled as historical. Earnings releases use LSEG RES events and require a company-confirmed next reported event; an empty response is not evidence of no earnings. Calendar coverage and exclusions appear in the diagnostics.

05 / CAPITAL FIRST

Reserve cash before collecting premium.

Constraint Rule
Illustrative starting capital $40,000 — not an assumed live balance
Exposure per ticker ≤ 30% of current equity
Total portfolio exposure ≤ 70% of current equity
Uncommitted cash reserve ≥ 30% of current equity
New contracts One per ticker
Borrowing / uncovered options Not allowed

Read actual cash, equity and the complete position book. Validate the account date and reconcile equity to cash plus marked positions before proposing trades.

Exposure = stock market value + short-put strike obligations, including pending puts. Covered calls do not add a second stock exposure.

Free cash = cash − existing and proposed put collateral. Expected premiums cannot fund the trade. When several puts qualify, allocate in ascending HV20 order (ticker breaks ties).

If existing holdings already breach a limit, no new orders are added. This version reports the breach; it does not force a liquidation.

07 / REPRODUCIBILITY

One engine. Two identical order paths.

Nightly, 10:00 PM New York

A timezone-aware GitHub Action retrieves LSEG inputs, reads Sanji state, evaluates the rules and validates orders. Exchange sessions—not weekdays alone—determine the next target date. GitHub scheduling can be delayed.

Explicit, auditable outputs

open_orders.json and mkt/open_orders.json are serialized from one array. Deterministic IDs prevent repeat fills. Raw market responses, exclusions and decisions are retained with a content hash.

Failure is visible

Missing account data, identifiers or required market data blocks order publication. Earlier files are preserved and labeled stale/unchanged. Only a validated no-trade run can write an empty queue.

The existing direct lseg.data Platform session uses GitHub Secrets. No running Workspace is required for that licensed connection. This is different from the desktop-proxy Eikon API. Credentials never enter this website or its JSON.

Deployment and account integration

Pages keeps the existing main-branch/root publishing source, preserving all previous assignments. The nightly job commits only generated public outputs and explicitly requests a Pages rebuild; it does not rely on a token-generated push triggering another workflow.

Sanji documents the order shape but not a complete account-file schema. The read-only account adapter is versioned and refuses unknown formats. No account has been initialized with illustrative cash. The course’s price_history.json must explicitly bind numeric conids to BAC, KO, PFE and F; company-name guesses are not accepted.

08 / EVALUATION & LIMITATIONS

A testable policy, not a profitability claim.

No performance results are reported for this new four-stock strategy yet. The existing single-stock backtests are separate assignments, not evidence for this implementation.

A future matched-window comparison would run this policy against a weekly Wheel without the extra filters, using the same initial capital, contract universe, fill assumptions and costs. Proposed metrics: total return, maximum drawdown, Sharpe ratio, premium income, assignment frequency, capital utilization and transaction costs.

Equity risk. Four stocks can decline together. Put assignment can create losses larger than premium income; calls cap upside and do not remove downside.

Execution risk. Short-dated gamma, overnight gaps, early assignment, dividends, spreads, fees and market-order slippage matter. No fills or fees are invented by the nightly planner.

Data risk. Observed option coverage may be incomplete. Greeks and earnings can be missing or revised. A fallback changes selection—not the facts—and historical replay would need point-in-time snapshots.
